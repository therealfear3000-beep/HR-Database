## Definition and scope
- **Claim:** Organizational Behaviour (OB) studies how individuals, groups, and organizational structure affect behavior at work, with an objective of improving organizational effectiveness.
- **Source:** Organisational Behaviour (Batch of 2027).pdf, p. 2; also Batch of 2026, p. 2.
- **Scope:** Definition attributed in the notes to Robbins.
- **Status:** verified against supplied page text, 2026-10-15.

## Managerial objectives of OB
- **Claim:** OB seeks to explain, predict and influence behavior in organizations.
- **Source:** Organisational Behaviour (Batch of 2027).pdf, p. 2; also Batch of 2026, p. 2.

## AMO model
- **Claim:** Employee performance can be understood in terms of ability, motivation and opportunity (AMO).
- **Source:** Organisational Behaviour (Batch of 2027).pdf, p. 2.
- **Status:** verified against supplied page text, 2026-10-15.

## Organizational Citizenship Behaviour (OCB)
- **Claim:** OCB refers to discretionary activity outside formal job requirements.
- **Source:** Organisational Behaviour (Batch of 2027).pdf, p. 2.
- **Status:** verified against supplied page text, 2026-10-15.

## ASA model
- **Claim:** Attraction–Selection–Attrition describes the processes through which people are drawn to organizations with similar people, selected for fit, and potentially leave if they do not fit; this can increase organizational homogeneity.
- **Source:** Organisational Behaviour (Batch of 2027).pdf, pp. 2–3.
- **Status:** verified against supplied page text, 2026-10-15.
